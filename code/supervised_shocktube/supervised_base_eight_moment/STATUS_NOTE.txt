Status note, 11 November 2026

The run records in accepted_state/evidence (TRAIN_RESULT.json, ASSESSMENT.json) carry the flags
paper_admission: false and all_criteria_pass: false. They record the position when the run was assessed:
two checks on the sampled physical measures at points not used by the refinement, with tolerance 0.1 %,
were not met. Criterion E4 (the 64 separate points) was set before the run; criterion E8 (64 points used
by no stage) was added after the first assessment (RUNS_DECLARED_2.json). The author subsequently decided
to report this state in the manuscript together with these limitations (section 5.2.7 and Appendix A.2).
The records are kept unaltered, except that the local folder named in the "run" field of the two
ASSESSMENT.json files is relocated to a symbolic root (RELOCATED_PATHS.json gives the original hashes).
